Privacy policy

1. Your game progress

The game saves a local guest identifier, progress, rank, in-game currency, energy, inventory and preferences on your device. These records let you resume play and keep earned rewards. You can play without creating a separate developer account. The game does not use a developer-operated game server.

2. Google Play Games

When you connect Google Play Games, the game uses your Play Games player identifier and display name to associate your progress with your Google account. Google Saved Games stores a copy of game progress so that you can restore it on another device. Leaderboards submit your game score; your Play Games profile and score may be visible to other players according to your Google settings. Google handles these services under its own privacy policy. Audio and display preferences remain on this device.

3. Rewarded ads and privacy choices

Optional rewarded ads use Google AdMob. Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares IP addresses (which can indicate general location), device/account identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Google processes this data under its privacy policy. Where applicable, Google's consent message appears before ads can be requested. When available, Settings > Play Games > Ad privacy choices lets you revisit those choices. You can continue normal play without choosing to watch a rewarded ad.

4. Permissions and data recipients

This game does not ask for your contacts, microphone, camera or precise location. Game data is sent to Google when using its cloud save, leaderboard and advertising services. 77 Game Studio does not operate a separate database of player accounts for this game. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you provide so that we can answer your request.

5. Retention and security

Local progress remains until you clear the game's app data or remove it; device or Google backup settings may keep a separate copy. Google retains cloud game data and advertising data according to its service policies and your account choices. Data exchanged by the Google SDKs is protected in transit. No system can guarantee absolute security. Support emails are kept while handling your request and any related obligations; contact us to request their deletion.

6. Delete data and manage choices

To remove this device's local progress, use Android Settings > Apps > Lancelot & Arthur > Storage > Clear data. This also removes locally earned progress. Google cloud progress may still be restored when you reconnect. To manage this game's Google Play Games data, visit https://play.google.com/games/profile, open Your Data, choose Delete Play Games Account & Data, and select this game under Delete individual game data. Google's deletion controls do not clear existing local copies on your devices. Manage your Play Games profile visibility and Android advertising identifier through Google's account and device settings. For other privacy or deletion requests, email j1120620314@gmail.com.
